## Short description

A desktop design studio that brings editable design tools and your own AI account into the same workspace.

## Founder story

I was tired of paying for Canva and an AI subscription, then still jumping between the two to finish a design. I kept thinking: why can't these be one workflow, with AI that I use on my own terms?

That's why I built Forma Studio.

It gives you a canvas where you can arrange photos, text, graphics, charts and editable templates. The AI works alongside those tools: you can give it a brief, refine a selection, or mark a specific part of an image with AI Pencil. Your business, mission and personal design preferences help guide the work instead of starting from scratch with every prompt.

Campaigns bring related designs into one place, with folders for different objectives and formats. You can provide the images you want used and keep working with the result in the editor.

Projects are saved on your computer. AI features use your own supported Codex account; model requests go to the provider, and its account requirements and usage costs still apply. Forma doesn't run an AI service on your behalf.

The Windows app is free to download. I'm sharing it because I wanted a more direct way to move from an idea to a design I could actually edit, without living between separate tools.
