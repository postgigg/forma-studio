What am I downloading?+

Forma Studio 1.6.1 for Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The installer includes the editor, local AI worker and Codex CLI. Open Forma from your desktop or Start menu. This release is unsigned.

Can I work without AI?+

Yes. Edit templates, photos, text, charts and 3D, then export PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, editable JSON, GIF or WebM. Manual editing and exports work offline. AI tools need your own supported Codex account and internet access.

Where does my work live?+

On your device. Export a Forma JSON file for an editable backup or to move projects. AI requests send your brief and relevant content to the provider. Forma runs no application or AI server on our end.

Can I use my own photos and fonts?+

Yes. Upload your images and TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2 fonts. Save your brand palette, mission and text kits, then carry that direction into your designs and AI briefs.

How do updates work?+

The desktop app checks GitHub Releases when you are online. Choose Download update, then Save & restart when ready. Designs stay on your device. Version 1.1 users need the current installer once to enable in-app updates.

When can I get the Mac app?+

The Mac edition is planned at $49 as a one-time payment, for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 12 or later. Distribution signing and payment delivery are being set up. Checkout is not open and no payment is collected here.
