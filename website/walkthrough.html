The walkthrough

Start here.

From opening Forma to making something your own.

Download for Windows
01

Install. Open. Create.

Download the Windows installer, run it and choose your installation folder. Open Forma Studio from your desktop or Start menu. No terminal, Node.js or Docker is needed for everyday use.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The current installer is unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

02

Make your first design.

Choose a template from Design, or start a blank canvas. Add text, photos and elements from the sidebar. Drag to arrange, use the handles to resize, and select several objects to move or group them together.

Right-click a selection for actions such as Improve with AI. Undo is always there while you explore.

Give it your direction.

Open Brand / Set up brand & style. Add your business, mission, palette and personal design preferences in one place. This gives the AI shared context for future requests.

03

Your AI.
Your direction.

Open Help / AI accounts & models. Choose Codex, Claude, Gemini or Kimi, using your own installed and signed-in CLI. Codex is included with Forma. Or choose Claude plans / Codex builds for a sequential handoff. Only one model runs at a time. A detected CLI still needs an authenticated account with available usage.

AI needs your own supported account, internet access and available provider usage. The brief and relevant content go to that provider; account costs are separate from Forma. Manual editing and exports work offline.

Connect your account.

In the desktop app, choose Sign in to Claude, Codex or Kimi. The sequential workflow shows both Claude and Codex buttons. For Claude, choose subscription sign-in or your existing API billing. Complete sign-in, then save the AI choice. Gemini uses its own CLI sign-in.

A brief that helps.

Describe the audience, purpose, format, main message and what must stay unchanged. Upload the images you want used. Review the result and any review warnings before applying it.

See the work being submitted.

In the same connection panel, open Peek under the commands to inspect submitted instructions, commands, actions and results. The activity view omits private reasoning and redacts recognized credentials.

Change one specific area.

Photo generation, AI Pencil and Magic Eraser use Codex native image generation. Select a photo, right-click and choose AI Pencil. Mark the area, describe the change, and review it in the context of the original image.

04

Build a campaign.

Open Campaigns in the editor header. Create the campaign around one objective, then use folders for different audiences, offers or subcampaigns.

Give each folder its intent, choose the formats and quantity, upload your images, then create the designs. Review and edit each result before exporting.

Keep the details editable.

Add charts and edit their data in the inspector. Use photo frames and crop controls, rotate 3D objects, and adjust individual text and graphic layers.

Give the idea
some room.

Choose New design / Whiteboard. Add photos, containers and sticky notes. Draw a connector between objects, or select two and connect them. Drag an end to reconnect it, or the middle handle to bend it.

Attached arrows follow objects as they move and resize. Routes avoid obstructions; Properties controls labels, colors, patterns and end markers. Notes and photos inside a container move together. Hold Space and drag to pan.

Use the existing marker, highlighter and stroke eraser. Add local comment pins, resolve them, and keep them with the editable backup. Comment pins stay out of finished artwork exports.

See the look.
Then choose.

Select a photo and open Properties / Photo filters. Each of the 28 looks previews your selected photo above its name, with its crop and current adjustments. Stack up to six looks, change strengths and brightness, reorder or reset them.

Previews are generated locally from small copies. Your original image stays editable.

05

Keep it.
Share it.

Your designs save on this device. Use Export / Forma JSON for an editable backup or to move a design to another computer. Import that file into Forma to keep working.

Export finished work as PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF. Use GIF for silent animation, or MP4 / WebM for movies with saved page audio. Select all pages to stitch them into one movie. Keep an editable JSON backup before moving or uninstalling the app.

Give it a soundtrack.

Choose Audio beside the page strip to record or import clips. Set trim and timeline positions to hundredths of a second. Speed and volume start at 100%. Split and join clips, download an MP3 or WAV mix, or choose Save audio & create movie. Speed changes also change pitch.

Stay up to date.

Open Help & settings in the tool rail to see your installed version, then choose Check for updates. The window’s Help / Check for updates menu opens the same updater. Download the update, then choose Save & restart when you are ready. Your designs remain on the device.

Versions 1.1.x need the current installer once to enable future in-app updates.

Get Forma

Your storage.
Your choice.

In the desktop app, open My designs / Cloud storage. Connect your own Wasabi account to back up designs, campaigns, uploads, fonts, text kits and your shared brand and style together.

Local saves continue independently. Cloud keys are encrypted on your computer; backup transfers go directly to your private Wasabi bucket. Wasabi bills your account separately.

Choose Back up now or enable automatic backups of changed saved work, up to every 15 minutes while Forma is open. Backups are compressed and limited to 256 MB of saved workspace data each. Built-in assets and AI credentials are not uploaded.

Connect your bucket.

  1. Create a Wasabi account and a private bucket. Keep public access off.
  2. Create a dedicated programmatic user with s3:ListBucket on that bucket and s3:PutObject / s3:GetObject on YOUR_BUCKET/forma-studio/backups/*. Delete access is not needed.
  3. Create the user's access key. In Forma, enter its bucket, region, matching HTTPS endpoint, access key and secret.
  4. Choose Connect & back up. Wait for the completed-backup confirmation.

Recover without losing current projects.

Choose Browse backups / Review restore. Restore adds designs and campaigns as new copies. You decide whether to use the saved brand and style. Unfinished AI work stays paused until you resume it. Finish or pause running campaigns first, then reopen the studio after restoring.

Disconnect keeps the existing backups in your account. Manage history and retention there. Review Wasabi’s storage minimums and retention billing.

Work together.
Keep it yours.

Open Work together in the desktop app. Start a session on your private Wi-Fi or Ethernet network, then copy the invitation to someone using Forma 1.10 or later.

  1. The other person chooses Join with a code and requests access.
  2. You approve them as a viewer, commenter or editor.
  3. Edit the same design, see selections and cursors, and leave object-attached comments in the existing Comments panel.
  4. Manage access or end the session whenever you are done.

Only the chosen design is shared. Transfers are encrypted and travel directly between computers. Each person saves a local copy; your other projects and AI accounts stay private.

The host must keep Forma open. This first version needs a reachable private network; it does not connect distant computers automatically.

A shared canvas.
Room for feedback.

Use the session bar to open Comments. Attach a note to a selected element, resolve feedback, and keep the discussion with the design.

Changes to different elements merge. When people change the same property from an old value, Forma keeps the first accepted value and explains the conflict. Your undo preserves other people’s independent changes.

Removing someone stops future access; copies already received remain on their computer. Ended sessions leave editable local files.
